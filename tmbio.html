Thomas Michael Mulligan
THOMAS M. MULLIGAN, PhD, is a retired faculty member of Brock University in St. Catharines, Ontario, Canada. He earned his PhD at Northwestern University (Evanston, Illinois) in the field of Philosophy and worked for a number of years as a manager and consultant in the software and manufacturing industries. His research interests include the philosophical foundations of business ethics, business purpose, the role of the humanities in business education, the future of work, and the nature of social progress.
